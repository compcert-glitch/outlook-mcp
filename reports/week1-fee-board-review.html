What the week actually shows

Five points worth the meeting's time. Everything below is arithmetic off the board, not interpretation of anyone's performance.

Concentration

Two teams carry 69% of the shortfall

T6 (NR, EB, BF) is −£89,561 and T3 (EP, CW, AL) is −£38,493. Between them that is £128,054 of the £186,474 gap against team targets. Fixing the other seven lines entirely would not close it.

Engine room

T6 is still half the practice

T6 posted £117,120 — 48% of all fee income this week — while running at 56.7% of its own target. It is simultaneously the largest earner and the largest absolute miss, so its target is the one that most needs testing against realistic capacity.

Zero lines

Two team lines produced nothing

T7 (JM) posted −£100 for the week — a credit or adjustment against no fees at all. T4 sits at zero income against a zero target: a whole team line on the board carrying neither work nor expectation.

Rhythm

Delivery is extremely lumpy

Thursday alone brought £111,626, against £31,908 on Wednesday and £24,175 on Friday — Thursday was 4.6× Friday. Fee posting is bunching rather than flowing, which makes any single day a poor read on the week.

Week ahead

The target rises as the gap opens

Week 2's board target is £422,287, up 20.4% on Week 1. Repeating Week 1's income would put the practice £176,013 behind for Week 2 alone, and roughly £280,000 behind across the fortnight.

Bright spot

One team beat its number

T1 (KP) posted £8,192 against £4,682 — 175% of target and the only line in surplus. Small in absolute terms, but worth asking what the pipeline looked like going into the week.

Queries on the board itself

Four things to settle before Week 2 is written up, because they change what the numbers mean.

  1. The team targets and the company target do not agree. The ten team weekly targets sum to £432,869, but the company target line reads £350,604 — a difference of £82,265, or 23%. Week 2 shows the same pattern: £559,217 against £422,287. Whichever is the real number, the practice is currently reviewing itself against two of them.
  2. Monday is blank on every row. No income and no target is recorded for Monday anywhere on the board. If that is a bank holiday or a non-posting day it should be struck through; if it is a missing day, the week's income is understated.
  3. Wednesday does not cross-cast. The team figures sum to £32,109 against a total line of £31,908 — £201 unaccounted for. Tuesday, Thursday and Friday all reconcile to the pound, so this is a single posting error rather than a systemic one.
  4. The monthly block is not covered by this review. The columns to the right of W4 carry the year to date and were not legible enough to reconcile. They should be pulled from the accounts system rather than read off the board before anyone quotes them.
